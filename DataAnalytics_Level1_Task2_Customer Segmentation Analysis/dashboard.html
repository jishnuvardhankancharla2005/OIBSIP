What to do for each segment

Four actions, grounded directly in the RFM profile of each cluster above.

1

High-Value Spenders — protect and retain, don't discount

This segment already proves it will spend big in a single order despite ordering only occasionally. A VIP program — dedicated support, early access, free premium shipping — protects that revenue better than a blanket discount would, which would just erode margin on demand that already existed.

2

Loyal Repeat Customers — cross-sell and reward

The most frequently ordering segment is the best candidate for loyalty points and bundle-based cross-sell campaigns. Small incentives compound here because they already return on their own.

3

New / Recent One-Time — convert the second purchase

These customers converted once, recently. The critical window is getting them to a second order — a time-limited "welcome back" offer or a post-purchase email sequence timed to a typical reorder window is the highest-leverage move for this segment.

4

Lapsed / At-Risk — light-touch reactivation only

Long time since last order and low historical spend make this the lowest-ROI segment to chase hard. A low-cost reactivation email is worth trying once or twice, but budget should not be pulled from the other three segments to chase this one.
